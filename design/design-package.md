# Catalyst Bible College — Design Package (Tier 1)

The single source for the build. Copy ships verbatim. Band ranges are starting points, validated by the flick test.

## Brand premise

Catalyst is the spark that starts the reaction. The film forges the college's circular seal out of fire and stone: one ember ignites, lava flows, and it cools into a mountain emblem that lasts. Stone is permanence, the mountain is the calling and the Sierra outside the door, the ember is the student. The page below the film answers the three real doubts in order: is the degree real, can we afford it, will I actually be prepared.

One call to action, everywhere: **Start Your Application** → https://connect.nelson.edu/apply/

- Label: THE MATH
- H2: Change the world. Skip the debt.
- Intro: Four years at a private Christian university routinely costs six figures. Catalyst was built to break that pattern.

## Form decision (honest)

No form. The single conversion is the Nelson application, which already exists and works. Phone and address for humans. Stated to the user at preview.

## Engineering standard (from the skill)

Blob-fetched video, rAF lerp that rests, gated seeks, DOM writes on change only, scrimmed paced bands, static end-frame hero at the five gates (file://, fetch fail, reduced motion, phone, error), page complete without the video. One index.html + assets/, no frameworks, no build step. Deploy folder is docs/ so GitHub Pages can serve it directly; review files stay outside docs/.
