## Applying to Catalyst Bible College

When applying to Catalyst Bible College, you will create an application with Nelson University first.

Choose "Create an Account" under First Time Users.

Complete the form that asks for your Email Address, First Name, Last Name and Birthdate.

Next, you will receive a temporary PIN that will be sent to your email. From there, you will be asked to create a password for your Nelson application account.

Once your password is completed, you will select Start New Application. The application type you will choose will be: Main Application and Undergraduate Partner Site Admissions Application. If you are applying for a graduate program, you will select Graduate Admissions Application.

You will then be taken to your live application. Under the tab "Applicant Information", you will select Visalia, CA // Catalyst Bible College from the drop down menu.

Everything that remains on the application will be personal and academic history and information. If you need assistance in completing the application, please contact our CBC offices.

### SOM Site Approval

Once your application is completed, every student will be presented to the Catalyst Bible College staff for approval. It will noted [sic] in your application's outstanding items as "SOM Site Approval." The CBC staff will determine if you are an eligible candidate for enrolling and notify Nelson of the decision for acceptance.
