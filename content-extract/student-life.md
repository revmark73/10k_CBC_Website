## Student Fee Structure

Starting in the Fall of 2025, each student will be asked to pay $2,000 a semester in student fees. This covers technology maintenance, administrative costs, partnership fees, student life activities, and practicum development. Those enrolled with Nelson University will have their student fees included in their bill from Nelson.

## Student Chapel (section heading from saved copy)

All on-campus students will be asked to attend one chapel service a week and sign up for a small group in their respective ministry area (i.e. Young Adults or Adults). Chapel services will traditionally be held on Wednesdays and last 60 minutes. Services will be devotional in nature and have guest speakers.

## Networking Opportunities (section heading from saved copy)

Students will be able to interact, network, and learn from prominent leaders across the country multiple times each semester. Students will also have the chance to engage with local community leaders during their time as students.

## Community Life (section heading from saved copy)

Monthly, students will be able to participate in a community-building activity such as bowling, laser tag, a movie, or a "family dinner."

## Leadership Labs and Crossfire (section heading from saved copy)

Each week, students will gain practical insights on future career preparation through Leadership Labs. In addition to Labs, students will participate in Crossfire discussion that challenge their worldview and encourages them to deep dive into the Bible.

## Student Housing

Catalyst Bible College Student Housing is available to enrolled CBC Students. In order to qualify for student housing, you must:

- Be an active, enrolled student with CBC
- Apply through the Catalyst Offices prior to the start of the semester
- Please note students who live an hour outside of campus receive priority placement in Student Housing
CBC has the ability to house full-time students, just minutes away from our Visalia campus. Students have a payment of $450/month including rent, utilities, and house furniture. Students will have one-two roommates when they live in CBC student housing. Students are not required to live in student housing while enrolled.

Contact the Catalyst Student Success Office for housing policies and payment information.
