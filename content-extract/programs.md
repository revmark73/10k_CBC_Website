## Catalyst Bible College is powered by Nelson University.

Visalia First offers Catalyst Bible College as a Partner Site in partnership with Nelson University. All graduating students will receive a diploma from Nelson University. www.nelson.edu

### History of Nelson Universtity [sic]

Nelson University (formerly Southwestern Assemblies of God University) is named in honor of founder PC Nelson. The University's vision of being a distinguished Christian university that equips Spirit-empowered leaders with career and ministry skills by providing affordable education, hands-on training opportunities, and innovative academic programs remains unchanged. Nelson University is accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC) to award associates, baccalaureate, master's and doctorate degrees. Degree-granting institutions also may offer credentials such as certificates and diplomas at approved degree levels. Questions about the accreditation of Nelson University may be directed in writing to the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges at 1866 Southern Lane, Decatur, Georgia 30033-4097, by calling 404-679-4500, or by using information available on SACSCOC's website www.sacscoc.org. For more information on Nelson's accreditation, you can also visit: www.nelson.edu/about/accreditation

## Lifestyle of Learning

Through Nelson University, Catalyst Bible College is able to offer six different undergraduate degree programs, as well as a variety of graduate degrees. Regardless of the degree a student chooses to pursue, every student will have the opportunity to take part in our "Lifestyle of Learning" initiative. Students will be invited to conferences and special lectures that create an atmosphere for them to collaborate, grow, and develop in their callings.

Click on the icon beside each program to learn more about it.

## Undergraduate Degree Programs

### Church Leadership (B.A.)

The B.A. in Church Leadership will equip students for modern partical [sic] ministry. Students will choose 24 hours of specialization in their degree. This is an on-campus only program.

General Education Units: 57 · Major Units: 45 · Elective Units: 18 · Total Program Units: 120

### Humanitarian Relief and Development (B.A.)

The B.A. in Humanitarian Relief and Development is a degree plan built in partnership with world humanitarian leader Convoy of Hope, emphasizing compassion-based initiatives, poverty alleviation, and transformational community development. This is an on-campus only program.

General Education Units: 57 · Major Units: 45 · Elective Units: 18 · Total Program Units: 120

### Business (B.A.)

The B.A. program provides students with a solid business foundation. Students can choose to minor in accounting, business administration, or marketing.

General Education Units: 51 · Major Units: 39 · Elective Units: 30 · Total Program Units: 120

### Human Services (B.A.)

The B.A. program provides students with the opportunity to pursue a career in criminal justice, social work, counseling, or psychology.

General Education Units: 51 · Major Units: 39 · Elective Units: 30 · Total Program Units: 120

### Education (B.A., Pre-Professional track)

The B.A. in Education (Pre-Professional track) equips students with the necessary skills, knowledge, and experiences to pursue professional careers or advanced study in the field of education.

General Education Units: 50 · Major Units: 61 · Elective Units: 9 · Total Program Units: 120

### Bible and Theology (B.A.)

The B.A. in Bible and Theology degree equips students with a deep understanding of scripture and theological principles, preparing them for impactful ministries and careers.

General Education Units: 57 · Major Units: 48 · Elective Units: 15 · Total Program Units: 120

### Interdisciplinary Studies (B.A.)

The B.A. in Interdisciplinary Studies program prepares you to think critically and solve problems by drawing from various fields, making you a more versatile professional.

General Education Units: 51 · Elective Units: 69 · Total Program Units: 120

## Graduate Degree Programs

### Educational Leadership (M.Ed.)

The Master of Education in Educational Leadership instructs aspiring school administrators in scientific research, pedagogy, and instructional technology. Total Program Units: 30

### Business Administration (M.A.)

The MBA program provides rigorous courses that develop your leadership skills and sharpen your business acumen. Total Program Units: 36

### Clinical Mental Health Counseling (M.S.)

The Clinical Mental Health Counseling (CMHC) is a graduate-level program focused on effectively equipping students to earn licensure as a professional counselor (LPC). The CMHC program also prepares students for the successful pursuit of doctoral studies in Counseling. CACREP Accredited. Total Program Units: 60

### Organizational Leadership (M.A.)

This degree will prepare you with advanced leadership skills for ministry and traditional workplace environments. Total Program Units: 36

### Master of Divinity (M.Div.)

The Master of Divinity offers a thesis and non-thesis option as well as specializations in Biblical Languages, Biblical Studies, Chaplaincy, Counseling, Family Ministries, Intercultural Studies, Global Compassion Ministries, Islamic Studies, Practical Theology, and Theological Studies. Total Program Units: 72

### Intercultural Studies (M.A.)

The Master of Arts in Intercultural Studies degree program integrates theological, missiological, and practical training to equip graduates with skills and tools to impact diverse groups of people, especially in cross-cultural ministries. Total Program Units: 36

### Practical Theology (M.A.)

The Master's degree in Practical Theology prepares you in the areas of biblical studies, theological understanding, and practical ministry. Total Program Units: 36

### Bible and Theology (M.A.)

The Master of Arts in Bible and Theology provides advanced knowledge in the study, interpretation, and application of the Word of God. Total Program Units: 36

### English (M.A.)

The Master of Arts degree in English provides graduate students with advanced training in English and its specialized disciplines. Total Program Units: 30

### Children and Family (M.A.)

The Master of Arts in Children & Family focuses on improving the lives of children, families, and communities, and is an excellent way for students to prepare for doctoral studies in Children and Family Studies. Total Program Units: 36

### History (M.A.)

The Master of Arts in History is designed to deepen students' understanding of historical events, themes, and methodologies, providing a strong foundation for pursuing a Ph.D. and a variety of career paths. Total Program Units: 30

## Post Graduate Degree Program

The Doctor of Ministry in Leadership (DMin) provides students the opportunity to gain advanced training in leadership, with special focus opportunities in communication and technology, cultural engagement, or general ministries.

### Business Administration (DBA)
