About Our Campus

From the very beginning, New Life has been about leading people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We exist to Love God and Lead People to Live a Better Story.

We are your neighbors, your friends and your co-workers, gathering together to explore God's goodness. We are a church where you can be yourself, build friendships, strengthen your marriage, watch your children grow, and bring friends and family. At New Life you have the opportunity to live a better story, an extraordinary story, for God.

Main Site

The main site of Catalyst Bible College is in Visalia, California. In 2022, Dr. Mark Merrill and a small team set out to help men and women called to ministry graduate as close to debt-free as possible. Today Catalyst offers a range of accredited degrees in a flexible setting. Students can study in a hybrid model, combining online coursework with in-person instruction, so their education fits the way they learn and the schedule they keep.
