Straight Answers

The questions parents ask.

Is this a real accredited degree?

Yes. Graduates receive a Nelson University diploma. Nelson is accredited by SACSCOC to award degrees from associate to doctorate.

What does it really cost?

$305 per credit hour, about $7,320 a year in tuition, plus a $2,000 per semester site fee. Federal financial aid applies, and more than $100,000 in scholarships is awarded every year.

Where do students live?

CBC housing sits minutes from the Visalia main campus: $450 a month with rent, utilities, and furniture included, one or two roommates. Living in housing is optional.

How do I apply?

Through Nelson University's application. Pick your campus from the site dropdown, like "Visalia, CA // Catalyst Bible College." The whole thing takes about 20 minutes, and our office walks you through it.

Can I visit first?

Yes. Call 559-733-9070 and we'll set up a visit at whichever campus is nearest you, or a meeting with admissions.
