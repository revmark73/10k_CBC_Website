The Forge

The Forge is Catalyst Bible College's hands-on ministry and leadership development experience, and it is a core requirement for all Church Leadership majors. It exists to intentionally shape, stretch and strengthen students for real-world ministry through Practicum-based training, mentorship and spiritual formation.

Students in The Forge are considered the leadership of the school. As such, they are held to a higher standard of spiritual maturity, character and conduct, both on and off campus. Forge students are expected to lead by example in classrooms, chapels, ministries and relationships, as visible representatives of Catalyst's mission.

Benefits of The Forge

  • Earn Scholarship Points toward tuition through consistent Practicum involvement
  • Eligibility for exclusive awards and recognitions at the Year-End Gala
  • Receive limited-edition Forge Team gear upon completing 96 Practicum hours each semester
  • Build a strong ministry resume through real-world experience in a church or leadership setting
  • Develop practical skills in communication, planning, discipleship, creativity, and more
  • Receive supervisor evaluations and coaching for future employment references
  • Access to exclusive leadership events, speakers, and guests when available
  • Opportunities to connect with local pastors, missionaries, creatives, and ministry leaders when available
  • Invitations to select conferences, trainings, or intensives through Catalyst's ministry partnerships when available
  • One-on-one check-ins with ministry supervisors and mentors
  • Weekly Mentorship Labs for spiritual formation and leadership growth
  • Intentional feedback to help you become who God is calling you to be

Student Requirements

  • Serve 84 verified Practicum hours per semester in a designated ministry area on or through campus
  • Participate in weekly Mentorship Labs, supervisor check-ins, and ministry responsibilities as assigned
  • Maintain a 70% average or higher in all academic courses
  • Remain in good standing with the Student Handbook, including lifestyle, language, and dress code expectations
  • Uphold professional standards in communication and conduct, including appropriate use of technology and boundaries
  • Submit time sheets and evaluations on time for credit and accountability
  • Demonstrate spiritual growth, teachability, and leadership development throughout the semester
  • Attend all required Forge events, including the Year-End Gala
  • Sign the Student Covenant each semester in order to be placed in Practicum
