Privacy Notice

Effective November 14, 2026 · Notice version 2026-11-14

This notice covers this website and the question form on it. It tells you what we collect, why, who sees it, and what you can ask us to do with it. If anything here is unclear, call us at 559-733-9070.

Who we are

Catalyst Bible College, 3737 S Akers Street, Visalia, CA 93277. Our students earn Nelson University degrees, but Nelson University does not run this website. When you apply, you apply on Nelson University's own site, and Nelson's privacy policy covers what you give them there.

What we collect

Only what you type into the question form:

  • your first and last name and your email address,
  • your phone number and high school graduation year, if you choose to give them,
  • the campus you're asking about, how you heard about us, and your question,
  • your answer to the age question, and the date and time you agreed to be contacted.

Browsing this site does not require you to give us anything. We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we don't put cookies on your device. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's internet address when a page opens, the same as for any website that uses them.

Why we collect it

To answer your question, and to talk with you about studying at Catalyst Bible College if you're interested. That's all. We don't sell your information, we don't trade it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who sees it

Our admissions and campus staff for the campus you picked, and the leaders who oversee all of our campuses. Your form goes into Catalyst Connect, our staff system, which is hosted for us by Base44. They store it for us and may not use it for anything else. We may share information if the law requires it.

Students under 18

Many people who ask about Catalyst are still in high school, and we take that seriously.

  • If you're under 13, please don't use the form. Ask a parent or guardian to call us instead. If we learn we've received information from someone under 13, we delete it.
  • If you're 13 to 17, you may use the form when a parent or guardian knows you're sending it. When we follow up, we'll include your parent or guardian.
  • A parent or guardian may ask to see what their child sent us, or ask us to delete it, by calling or writing to us.

How long we keep it

We keep a question for up to two years after our last contact with you. If you enroll, it becomes part of your student file. If you don't, we delete it after those two years, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices

  • Ask what information we have about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete it, or to stop contacting you.

To do any of these, call 559-733-9070 or write to Catalyst Bible College, 3737 S Akers Street, Visalia, CA 93277. We'll confirm it's really you before we change or share anything, and we'll respond within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Your form travels over an encrypted connection. Only signed-in staff with a need to see it can open it, and every change a staff member makes is logged.

"Do Not Track" signals

Because this site doesn't track you across other websites, it treats every visit the same way whether or not your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll post the new version here with a new date. The version date above is saved with each question, so we always know which notice you agreed to.
