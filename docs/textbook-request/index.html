Textbook Request

Requests are closed for the Fall 2026 semester.

Requesting a textbook does not guarantee that Catalyst will be able to purchase it for you. If you have questions about your request, visit the Student Life Office.

This service is only for on-campus Catalyst Bible College students during the Fall and Spring semesters. The most we can cover is $50 per student. First priority goes to on-campus Church Leadership and Theology undergraduates. Requests close on the day intake packets are due.

Textbook funds are graciously donated by the Visalia First Men's Ministry.
