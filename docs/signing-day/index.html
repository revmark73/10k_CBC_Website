Apply to Catalyst Bible College

Start your application

  1. When applying to Catalyst Bible College, you create an application with Nelson University first. Choose "Create an Account" under First Time Users.
  2. Complete the form that asks for your email address, first name, last name and birthdate.
  3. You'll receive a temporary PIN by email. From there, you'll create a password for your Nelson application account.
  4. Select Start New Application. Choose Main Application and Undergraduate Partner Site Admissions Application. For a graduate program, choose Graduate Admissions Application.
  5. On your live application, under the "Applicant Information" tab, select your campus from the site menu, such as Visalia, CA // Catalyst Bible College.
  6. The rest of the application asks for your personal and academic history. If you need help completing it, contact our CBC offices.
  7. Once your application is complete, it is presented to the Catalyst Bible College staff for approval. It shows in your application's outstanding items as "SOM Site Approval." CBC staff determine whether you are an eligible candidate and notify Nelson of the decision.
